In the presence of heavy tails, the sample mean is no longer an efficient measure of central tendency. As such, the quality of standard regression estimation techniques, such as OLS, can degrade significantly when this is the case. Different estimators have been studied in order to address this issue, with M-Estimators (\cite{huber1992robust}) being one of the most frequently used alternatives.

Another technique that has gained traction in the machine learning literature is the Median-of-Means (MoM) estimator (\cite{lecue2020robust}), particularly due to its Subgaussian nature.
